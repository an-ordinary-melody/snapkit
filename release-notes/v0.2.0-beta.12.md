Twelfth beta of Snapkit, a fast screenshot tool for macOS and Windows.

## What's new since beta.11

- **Compress images.** Smaller PNGs when you copy or save: Standard keeps the size and reduces colors (text stays sharp, often 60–80% smaller); Smallest also brings Retina / HiDPI captures back to 1×. Pasting keeps the savings in browsers, Slack, Discord, Notion and Office. Turn it on in Settings → General → After a capture; the editor has a Compress switch with the file size.
- **Capture last area** (`⌥⇧X`, Windows: `Alt+Shift+X`). Reopens the area you last copied or saved, or the whole screen after a full-screen capture. Turn on "Copy last area right away" to skip capture mode.
- **Ruler tool** (`M`). Measures in image pixels; part of the image when you copy or save. Off by default: turn it on in Settings → Toolbar.
- **macOS: Snapkit shows in the Dock while Settings is open**, and goes back to the menu bar when you close it.
- **macOS: frosted glass app icon.**
- **Windows: the floating preview stays on top** even when Windows drops its on-top flag.

Beta users get a notification about this release; download and install it over the old one.

## Shortcuts

`⌥⇧Z` (Windows: `Alt+Shift+Z`) capture an area · `⌥⇧X` last area · `⌘⇧9` full screen · `⌘⇧1` history · `⌘⇧2` settings (Windows: Ctrl instead of ⌘). All changeable in Settings → Shortcuts.

## Install

**macOS (Apple Silicon only):** download `Snapkit-0.2.0-beta.12-mac-arm64.dmg`, open it and drag Snapkit to Applications.
Snapkit isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS blocks the first launch:
open Snapkit once, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
(Or in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Snapkit.app`.)
Then allow **Screen Recording** when asked, and quit and reopen Snapkit. After updating, macOS may ask for Screen Recording again.

**Windows (x64):** run `Snapkit-0.2.0-beta.12-win-x64-setup.exe`. SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher: click **More info → Run anyway**.
If something misbehaves, the log is at `%APPDATA%\Snapkit\logs\main.log`; please attach it to a bug report.
